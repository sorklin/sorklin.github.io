How the number gets built

Five steps, no color picker.

Scope the real assets

What actually hurts if it goes sideways, and how much it costs when it does.

Name the scenarios

“Ransomware of the backup appliance”, not “advanced persistent threat”.

Calibrate the ranges

min / expected / max from the people who know the system — plus the sceptic in the room.

Simulate

Tens of thousands of trials. A distribution, not a single optimistic cell.

Deliver the number

Annualised loss, exceedance curve, and the three things worth funding first.
